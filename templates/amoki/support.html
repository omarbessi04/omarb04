Support

Welcome, and thank you for writing.

Amoki is a private journal for the time you spend with the people who matter. You log what you did, you both score it, and the years quietly add up.

If something is broken, confusing, or missing, I want to hear about it. A message from someone actually using the app is the best part of building it, so please don’t worry about whether your question is too small to send.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Amoki works fully offline and needs no sign-up. An anonymous account is created only if you choose to share a space with someone.

How do I share a space with someone?

Open Settings and tap Invite. You get an eight-character code to send or read out. The other person goes to Spaces → Join a shared space and enters it. Codes last seven days.

Can I score an outing for someone who doesn’t have the app?

Yes. Hand them your phone and let them enter their own score. It stays theirs, and it syncs properly if they join later.

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on sync, and even then only the spaces you have shared.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Clear all data. You can remove it from this iPhone only, or everywhere.

Why can’t I see the score?

Neither of you sees a score until you have both entered one.
