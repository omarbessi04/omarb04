Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Amoki app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Ómar Bessi Ómarsson (collectively, the “Application”). Ómar Bessi Ómarsson is hereby referred to as the “Service Provider”.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as

  • Your device’s Internet Protocol address
  • The screens of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on them
  • When the Application is opened, closed, installed and updated
  • Your device model, mobile operating system and Application version
  • Reports of errors and crashes that occur while you use the Application

What you write in the Application — your spaces, outings, chapters, notes, places, prices, grades, ideas and photos — is stored on your device and is not collected by the Service Provider unless you turn on sync. See “Sync and your content” below.

Analytics and error reporting

The Service Provider uses PostHog to understand how the Application is used and to learn about crashes. PostHog is a third-party analytics provider acting as a processor on the Service Provider’s behalf.

PostHog receives a record of which actions occur — that an outing was created, that a grade was saved, that onboarding finished — together with plain attributes such as whether an outing had a photo, or whether a space is for one person, two or a group. It also automatically records the screens you open, when the Application is opened and closed, and any errors or crashes.

PostHog does not receive what you write. No outing titles, notes, places, names, ideas, photos or grade values are sent to it.

The Application does not record your screen and does not capture your taps. Session replay and element-interaction capture are both disabled.

PostHog assigns a random identifier to your installation. If you turn on sync, these events are also associated with the anonymous account identifier described below.

Sync and your content

Sync is off by default. You turn it on yourself, either in Settings or by inviting someone into a space.

When you turn it on, the Application creates an anonymous account for you. This requires no email address, no password and no name, and it is not linked to your Apple ID.

From then on, the contents of your synced spaces are stored by Supabase, the Service Provider’s backend provider, so that the people you share a space with can read them. This includes outing titles, dates, places, prices, who paid, notes, grades, date ideas, the display names you give the people in a space, and any photos you attach.

Photos are stripped of their metadata before upload, including any location recorded by your camera.

Only the members of a space can read that space’s contents, and this is enforced by the database itself rather than only by the Application. If you invite someone into a space, they can read and edit everything in that space, including outings you created. Spaces you have not shared remain private to you.

If sync is on, the Application also stores your device’s push notification token so that you can be told when someone you share a space with has graded something. That token is readable by no other user.

Third Party Access

The Service Provider uses a small number of third-party processors, and the data they receive is not aggregated or anonymized:

  • Supabase — stores the contents of your synced spaces, your photos, your anonymous account and your push notification token, when sync is on
  • PostHog — receives the analytics events and error reports described above, associated with a per-installation identifier and, once sync is on, with your anonymous account identifier
  • Apple — delivers push notifications through the Apple Push Notification service

The Application also uses Google AdMob to show a banner in the Log’s List and Calendar views and sponsored cards in Ideas and Album. It does not send your journal content, grades, names or photos to AdMob. Google’s SDK may collect IP addresses (including approximate location), device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information for advertising and analytics. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy.

The Application gathers ad consent before requesting ads and asks for Apple’s tracking permission before accessing the advertising identifier. Declining tracking permission does not prevent ads where Google permits ad requests. Where available, use Settings → Ad privacy settings to change your consent; tracking permission can also be changed in iOS Settings. Google processes advertising data under its own policies. The Application has no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Opt-Out Rights

In Settings, under “Clear all data”, you can delete your data on this iPhone only, or delete it everywhere — the second also removes it from the Service Provider’s backend. Leaving a space you were invited into removes only you from it. Turning sync off in Settings stops your content being transmitted.

You can also stop further collection by uninstalling the Application. Uninstalling will stop the Application from collecting data from your device, but it does not by itself delete information that has already been transmitted to the Service Provider or to third parties.

To request deletion of your personal data, to withdraw consent, or to exercise any of your rights, contact the Service Provider at amoki@omarbessi.com.
