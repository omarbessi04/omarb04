Translation Project

I was tired of not having a specific project to work on when it came to my Japanese study, and when I was biking to school I wished I understood the songs in the playlist I was listening to. I thought, “I could just translate them. How hard can it be?” I hand-translated the lyrics, wrote down how long that took me along with some other things, and charted the effort.
