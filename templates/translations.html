Ten songs in, I put this down to build Amoki. The data below is complete for those ten, the kanji explorer works on all of them, and the next song is already queued for when I pick it back up.

Part language-learning, part analysis - I hand-translate the lyrics of my favourite band and chart the effort as I go.

- Google Sheets: primary data source for study times and metadata.

- Spotify API: song names and track lengths.

- kanji-data (JLPT, frequency and stroke data) for the kanji explorer.

- Flask (Python), HTML / CSS, JavaScript.

Asian Kung-Fu Generation is a Japanese alt-rock band from Yokohama - and my favourite band. Translating their lyrics is how I practise the language and scratch the itch to measure things. Here’s how a song goes:

  1. Write the lyrics out by hand, highlight the words I don’t know, and break them apart. Example.
  2. Run a script to pull out features - track length, unique character count, the JLPT level of every kanji, minutes spent.
  3. Look for patterns: am I getting faster, and which songs fought back hardest?
